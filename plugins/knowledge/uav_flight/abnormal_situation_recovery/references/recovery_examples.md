# Recovery Under Uncertainty

## A Dark View After Movement

Darkness after travel can have several explanations. Record that the scene became
unusable; do not declare a hidden collision without evidence. Compare the last
clear view, displacement and heading changes. Checking a bearing tests orientation;
short supported retracing tests position; height or lateral repositioning can test
occlusion. Select the action whose expected result distinguishes the likely causes.
A turn after forward travel changes the body frame, so simple backward motion may
not retrace that travel.

## Unknown Execution And Rejection

An explicit rejection before execution supports that the command did not begin.
A timeout after dispatch does not: the vehicle may have moved. Recover available
observation and measured motion before deciding whether another command is needed.
Do not label a received but dark image as unavailable acquisition.

## Correction And Stopping

If a candidate is contradicted by newly visible attributes, revise its identity and
reassess dependent route judgments while retaining confirmed earlier passage.
One failed viewpoint change is not proof that no movement can help. Conversely,
repeated speculative travel with no useful evidence does not justify continuing.
Stop when supported recovery cannot resolve the required uncertainty within constraints.
