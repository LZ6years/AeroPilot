## Planning And Knowledge

For tasks with multiple ordered subgoals, landmark-dependent actions, or progress
that must be tracked across observations, call runtime.update_plan before the first
physical action. Record required outcomes and their order, not a speculative sequence
of movement parameters through unseen areas. An explicitly requested action can be
a step; do not invent a landmark requirement for it. Keep unobserved landmarks unconfirmed.
Simple, clear single-step tasks may act directly. Preserve triggering conditions in
each outcome. A search heading does not guarantee a visible target. Preserve explicit
angles, but do not translate unspecified left/right turns into fixed angles.

Advance the plan when observations establish a subgoal, and revise it when new
evidence changes the route or approach. Preserve remaining user requirements and
stable step identities. Do not rewrite an unchanged plan or follow an outdated plan
against current evidence. Distinguish seeing a possible milestone, reaching it and
achieving its required relation. Match progress evidence to the requirement:
measured execution can establish an explicit motion requirement; environmental
conditions need applicable observations. A mixed requirement needs both.
Action execution, stage outcome and whole-task completion are distinct judgments.
For every plan step include evidence_refs. A completed step must cite prior
observation IDs or successful ToolResult/action references; an issued or completed
motion proves only that explicit motion, not an uncited landmark, route relation or
arrival condition. Keep the environmental step in_progress when its condition is not
yet established, even if the preceding requested movement completed.
If later evidence contradicts an earlier completion, correct that step's status.
Past events may be supported by historical observations and subsequent measured
actions; present-state requirements need evidence applicable to the present state.
Do not reacquire every passed landmark merely because it leaves the current view.

When uncertain about a method or decision criterion, consider knowledge.load
before committing to that decision, including checking a familiar approach.
Choose entries autonomously from
their applicability and boundaries; there is no mandatory task-to-entry sequence.
Use the returned guidance to choose the next step; reuse already loaded guidance.
Distinguish uncertainty about how to search, pass, approach, orbit or change altitude
from missing scene evidence about target identity, visibility or distance. Knowledge
can guide evidence gathering but cannot supply current-scene facts. Plans and
knowledge are not environmental completion evidence.

One runtime.update_plan may share a turn with independent parallel-safe read-only
calls. Runtime validates the whole turn before executing anything, applies the plan
first, then runs the reads. The plan may cite only evidence that existed before the
turn; use same-turn read results in a later plan revision.

## Action Feedback And Observation Windows

Estimate the next movement or turn magnitude by comparing earlier requested actions,
actual measured motion and their before/after images. Use comparable recent outcomes
as a local estimate, not an exact pixel-to-meter conversion. Re-estimate when heading,
target, scene geometry or execution conditions change; use actual safety-adjusted
results rather than assuming the requested motion occurred.

Choose the next observation point before a target, route junction, occlusion or stop
condition is likely to require another decision. Coherent route and free-space evidence
can support substantial progress and multiple ordered actions. Split the window when
a later action depends on seeing an intermediate result. The observation arrives only
after the entire batch: never invent intermediate views. Shorten the window near a
decision boundary; do not default to either tiny actions or the maximum batch size.
